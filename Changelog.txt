Scorching Wide V1 Changes:

- The original Wide packaged with Elemental Long received some compensation for being stuck with affinity focus, getting bloated uncharged shell MVs
	- Wide will receive some light nerfs to shelling to make up for some of the buffs it will be receiving with this mod
		- Uncharged Shells: -2 MVs at all levels
		- Wyvern Fire: -3 MVs at all levels
		- Fullburst Modifier: -4% --> -10%
		- WSFB Modifier: -14% --> -20%

- Wide now gains a Heat Gauge:
	- Native Razor Sharp at all levels of Heat
		- Yellow: Level 1
		- Orange: Level 2
		- Red: Level 3
	- Gain MV buffs as your blade heats up
		- Orange: +4 MVs to Lance attacks
		- Red: +8 MVs to Lance attacks
	- Wyvern's Fire to activate Scorching Blade
		- During Scorching Blade your heat will be locked
			- Use the white bar to track heat buildup for the next Scorching Blade
			- Use the black bar to track your locked heat (current heat level = max(white, black)
			- Use the blue bar to track your remaining time in Scorching Blade
		- Additionally during Scorching Blade, your lance attacks will gain a secondary damage rider with some unique properties
			- Orange: 8 MV + 20 Fire Ele
			- Red: 16 MV + 40 Fire Ele
			- Scorching Blade rider damage scales with Raw, Affinity, Critical Boost, and Sharpness
			- Scorching Blade penetrates the toughest hides, the raw portion is calculated as always hitting a wounded 50 HZV


###PLEASE NOTE### The script for Scorching Blade's rider damage hijacks the Scorcher skill.  If you have no Scorcher equipped it will add Scorcher to two 
armor pieces and modify the code, if you have Scorcher 1 already equipped its damage will be averaged into Scorching Blade while its active, regardless of
heat level. HOWEVER, there is execution time required, starting as soon as the game sees a Wide Gunlance equipped.  Give it a second when you do so, if you
don't see Scorcher on your skills then you clicked through before the lua added them in
